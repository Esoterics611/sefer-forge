Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Sefer Forge ("the app") is an open-source publishing tool maintained by Ronnie Rendel. This policy explains what the app does with information from Google accounts that sign in to it. The app is used by its owner to publish the project's own videos to the project's own YouTube channel; it is not offered as a service to the public.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By signing in to it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, you are asked to allow two permissions:

  • YouTube (youtube): to upload videos to your channel and set their title, description, tags, language and privacy; to create playlists and add the uploaded videos to them; and to read your channel's name and its list of uploaded videos, so the app can tell what it has already published.
  • Google Drive, files made by the app (drive.file): to create a folder and copy the project's video files into it. This permission lets the app see only files it created; it cannot see or change anything else in your Drive.

The app does not access your email, contacts, comments, subscribers, analytics, or any video or file it did not create.

What the app stores

  • The sign-in token. After you sign in, Google gives the app a refresh token. It is kept only in the computing environment where the app runs (a file in the user's home folder, or an environment variable set by the owner). It is never committed to the source code, shared or sent anywhere other than to Google.
  • The publishing record. The project keeps a list of the videos it has published: each video's YouTube ID, the date it went up and its playlist. This list is part of the project's source code.

The app does not collect, sell or share personal information, and does not use advertising, analytics or tracking.

Who receives information

Information goes only to Google (YouTube and Google Drive), to carry out the actions above. No third party receives any data from the app.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Removing access and deleting data

  • You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google account's Third-party apps & services page (also reachable at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). The stored token then stops working.
  • Videos, playlists and Drive files the app created stay in your account until you delete them in YouTube Studio or Google Drive.
  • To have the stored token deleted, or with any question about this policy, contact the maintainer by opening an issue at github.com/Esoterics611/sefer-forge/issues. Stored information is deleted within 7 days of a request.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date above.
