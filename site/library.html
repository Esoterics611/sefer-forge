Every work so far, with its pages and its PDFs. Each one is made the same way: the text quoted exactly, every source it cites set in beside it, and the explanation written to be read.

The morning prayer after the Arizal

כוונות תפילת השחר על פי האר"י ז"ל

Each step of Shacharit, the Arizal's words set in exactly as printed, and beside each idea, under בנפש האדם, what it is in the one who prays. 8 of its 13 sections are written: 67 pages, 110 source passages. A second level, after the Rashash, is being written on top of the first.

The garments of the soul

לבושי הנשמה · לשון אדמו"ר נ"ע בעצמו

A short gloss in the Alter Rebbe's own words, explained note by note in a 24-page pamphlet; its reading pack sets out the 26 passages of Etz Chaim and Pri Etz Chaim it cites; and a four-page class handout, in English and Hebrew.

What comes next

  • Research and explanation. The writings of the Arizal and the discourses of Chassidus explained from their sources, every passage quoted exactly, checked before print.
  • Tefillah with meaning. The whole morning prayer, then weekday, Shabbat and festival prayers, at more than one level, for a beginner and a scholar.
  • Life in Torah and mitzvot. Tzitzit, tefillin, the blessings, the Shema: what each is, from the sources, and what it gives the one who keeps it.
  • Translation. English and French first, then Spanish, Russian and more, each checked against the Hebrew so nothing is lost or added.
  • Teaching packages. For each text, what a teacher can use tomorrow: the explanation, its sources, a class handout and a narrated lesson.
  • Open technology. The tools behind it, built in the open: reading sefarim from photographs, Hebrew speech-to-text, a lexicon of the terms.
