# Student read — שער היחוד פרק כח (prompts/student-read.md)

Read 2026-11-21, the built biur.md, by the writer reading as the student.

- The פרק is the נמשל of פרק כז; each step tied back there (the end of the form becoming the matter; "תלת שליטין"; "לאחזאה לאנהגא").
- §1: why a light that is only "appearance and colour" is still "בלי מה" said in plain words with the text's apple: the white shows the sweetness; tied to פרק יט ("כגוון התפוח").
- §3: "מהתעבות האור נעשה הכלי כמ"ש בע"ח" set in from Etz Chaim (6.3.1: the vessels were "בבחי' האור היותר עב וגס", joined in the light and not yet seen).
- §4: "two kinds of names" laid out (before Atzilut, the source; after, the vessels), and why intending the source reaches the vessel.
- §5: "כלי ההבטה" glossed as an instrument of seeing (a spyglass), so the parable of the eye reads.
