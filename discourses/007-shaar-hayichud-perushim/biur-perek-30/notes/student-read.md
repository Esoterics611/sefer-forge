# Student read — שער היחוד פרק ל (prompts/student-read.md)

Read 2026-11-21, the built biur.md, by the writer reading as the student.

- §1: the question ("how can a vessel remain without light, if it is made from the light?") stated in plain words, answered from פרק כט, and set in from Torah Or (מקץ), which gives the same answer in the Alter Rebbe's words.
- "דרכו של איש" completed from the Gemara (קידושין ב, ב, "לחזר על אשה") so the image reads: the light seeks the vessel.
- "אתה סתר למפרע דרת"ס" opened: סת"ר is רת"ס read backwards.
- The three levels laid out in one table across light, חסד, heart, liver and mitzvah, so §§2–4 are read against it.
- "קיסטא דחיותא" glossed from Shaar HaKavanot ("מדת החיים") in a footnote.
- רמ"ח: tied to the arithmetic in פרק כז.
