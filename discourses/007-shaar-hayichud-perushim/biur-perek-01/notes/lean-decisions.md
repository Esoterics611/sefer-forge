# Decisions on every gloss (skill lean-biur, the middle way), שער היחוד פרק א, 2026-11-24

Redone from the original ביאור of 2026-11-21 (124 glosses, before the first lean pass of the morning, which
cut to 30 and took the frame with it). Each gloss: חוזר (repeats the text: cut, its punctuation kept),
מבאר (explains what the student would not get alone: kept or trimmed), קושר (ties the stretch to the
argument: kept, short), מקור (an address and its point), מושג (a footnote on a term), מצביע (where a כנ"ל or
a parenthesis points). The sentence breaks alone (a period or comma between stretches) are not listed.

## The frame (not reviewed by lean-biur; restored, then every sentence made whole)
- מבוא: its second paragraph restored (the chapter's points). "הקונטרס השני" → "קונטרס" (the count is not
  in the text or its notes). "ועולה אל מקורו" given its subject; "מקומו של הדעת: אינו..." as a whole sentence.
- טבלת סדר המדרגות: stays out (Ronnie removed the table of levels in the booklet model; the text's own §14).
- סיכום restored. Whole-sentence fixes: "הזרם הצר שבאמצע" → "הזרם שהוא עיקר הנהר" (the text's words, "שבאמצע"
  was added); "מעיינות החכמה ייפתחו מלמטה ומלמעלה" → gates above, springs below, as the Zohar; "הברק" given
  what it is ("השכל החדש יוצא כברק"); "כמשה" → "ועל משה, שהוא הדעת, נאמר שזכה לבינה".
