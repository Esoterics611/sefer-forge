# Student read — שער היחוד פרק מד (prompts/student-read.md)

Read 2026-11-22, the built biur.md, by the writer reading as the student.

- "ק"ך צירופים" stands bare in the text; the עץ חיים passage set in below it (שער הזיווגים פ"ו) shows the 120 as four times thirty, and that they are the סמ"ך of בינה. Enough for the student; nothing added.
- "מאמר סתום ומאמר פתוח": the student meets the phrase cold. The gloss gives שבת קד, א in a note and recalls פרק לז (לאה ורחל), so the pair is anchored before the chapter turns it into the two forms of the מ"ם.
- "מ"ם דלמרבה": a student may not know the closed מ"ם in the middle of the word. The gloss says so (ישעיה ט, ו, "לם רבה"), and the זהר וילך passage shows "מ"ם פתוחה, מ"ם סתומה" with the verse. Kept.
- The turn from the מקיף to "אהבה התלויה בדבר" is abrupt in the text (a parenthesis). The gloss gives the משנה's words, "בטל דבר, בטלה אהבה", so the point (what remains after the love fades is the מקיף) reads.
- "מים דניידי", "דאגלידי מיא וקיימא": Aramaic; glossed as moving water and water frozen and standing, with עירובין מו, א for "ניידי". "השג יד" glossed from the משנה in ערכין, so "השגה" as grasping by hand is clear.
- "כתנות עור" and "חשמל" are promised for later ("כמשי"ת"); the gloss says only what this chapter says. A student will wait for פרק מה. Left so.
- The table "מ"ם סתומה ומ"ם פתוחה" sums the chapter; its last row ("המשל") puts ה"ג מנצפ"ך and "קול התור" under the open מ"ם, as the text does (the הארת המקיף to divide the combinations through ה"ג מנצפ"ך). Checked against the text: holds.
