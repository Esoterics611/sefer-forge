# Student read — שער היחוד פרק יח (prompts/student-read.md)

Read 2026-11-21, the built biur.md, by the writer reading as the student.

- "רצון לרצון" is the word the whole פרק turns on; glossed where it first comes in the text (§2: a will born of the will, toward one thing), and §3 ends by saying in plain words what is fixed (the essential will) and what is created (the רצון לרצון).
- §1: "אדם דבריאה" would stop a student (בריאה is below אצילות, א"ק above it). Etz Chaim set in: "ובבחי' היותו שניות לא"ס נק' אדם דבריאה עם שהוא קודם אצילות".
- §5: "מבחי' אחרונה שברצון הקדום" tied back to §1, so the two places the text says it read as one point.
- §6: "וחשב לעשות" given with its verse (ירמיה כו, ג); the Rambam's words in a footnote, from the halacha itself.
- Checked: "מקור כל הרצונות" ties back to פרק יז; "מח"ס" opened as the editor's manuscript reads it (במחשבה סתימאה).
