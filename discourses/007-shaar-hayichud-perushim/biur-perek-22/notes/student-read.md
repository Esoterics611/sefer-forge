# Student read — שער היחוד פרק כב (prompts/student-read.md)

Read 2026-11-21, the built biur.md, by the writer reading as the student.

- The פרק has two kinds of inclusion and they are easy to confuse; laid out in a table before the text (ביטול דבר בהיפוכו; חסד שבגבורה; גבורה שבחסד; the points that cannot be included), each with the text's own example.
- §3: "גבורה שבחסד" is named first in the text for the דין that strengthens חסד, then "חסד שבגבורה" for the reverse; the explanation follows the text's naming and says in each case which is the garment and which the inside ("פעולתו... ופנימיותו").
- §4: "כי הוא יך וירפאנו" is the text's wording; the verse (הושע ו, א) given as it stands, "כי הוא טרף וירפאנו, יך ויחבשנו".
- §5: Gabriel's two deeds each given with the Gemara (יומא עז, א; פסחים קיח, א; סנהדרין צה, ב); Pesachim has Gabriel offering to save Abraham, as quoted, not more.
- §6: "יהי רצון מלפניך" tied to פרק כא; "עומק טוב ועומק רע" to ספר יצירה א, ה.
