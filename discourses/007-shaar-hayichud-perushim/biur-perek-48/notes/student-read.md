# Student read — שער היחוד פרק מח (prompts/student-read.md)

Read 2026-11-22, the built biur.md, by the writer reading as the student.

- "באות הי'" is the text's pointer to פרק י (checked: "חפץ חסד" in the עצמות, and "אנא אמלוך"); the gloss names the chapter.
- "י' פסוקים מלכיות": the gloss gives the משנה's words, "אין פוחתין מעשרה מלכיות", so the student knows the practice before reading its meaning.
- "ויתנו לך כתר מלוכה" is a line of the prayer; the text cites only those words and the gloss adds none of the prayer. What the gloss says of it (the kingship is built from what the receivers give) is the text's next lines ("תלוי רק בביטול האנשים כשירוממוהו").
- The core (sections 3–4) is a plain parable and reads without help: חסד arises in the self even with no one to receive it; התנשאות does not arise without another. The table sets the two side by side.
- "אין מלך בלא עם" is given its source (רבנו בחיי, בראשית לח, ל); the עץ חיים passage set in (the names, "אדון", that cannot be said without creatures) shows the same thought in the Kabbalah's own reasoning for creation.
- "כנשר שהוא רחמני": the editor points to the ירושלמי, פאה פ"א ה"א; the eagle as merciful was not found there on Sefaria (see check.md). The gloss gives the text's words only.
- "גמלתהו טוב" and the root of the nations "שהפרידן מאחדותו", for the sake of תפארת: a pointer "במ"א"; the gloss gives the verse and the text's reason (תפארת is only before a stranger), no more.
- "נעוץ תחלתן בסופן" is given in ספר יצירה's words, both halves.
