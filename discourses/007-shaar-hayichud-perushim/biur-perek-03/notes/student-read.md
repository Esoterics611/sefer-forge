# Student read — שער היחוד פרק ג (prompts/student-read.md)

Read 2026-11-21, the built biur.md, by the writer reading as the student. Fixes applied in biur.src.md, rebuilt.

- §2 fn: the footnote said the divine force is likened "to force and not to light", while §5 then lists "כח הפועל" among the examples of התפשטות beside the sun's light: a student would see a contradiction. Its source is a modern note (מראי מקומות לתורה אור) and the Shulchan Aruch's words already explain "לשון כח". Applied: the footnote taken out.
- §5: "כח הפועל" listed with זיו השמש and אור הנר as התפשטות, though it is the subject of the chapter: a student would ask whether it is the same. Applied: one sentence pointing out that the example's own term is among them.
- §8: "מכ"ע" after "ממכ"ע" everywhere else: a student does not know whether it is another term. Applied: "בהשגה זו", the text's own words, tie it to ממלא כל עלמין.
- Left: "ממלא כל עלמין" glossed briefly ("הממלאת כל עולם ומחיה אותו מבפנים"), as the text itself explains it at once ("כח הפועל האלקי בנפעל"); its contrast, סובב כל עלמין, is not in this chapter.
- The parenthesis opened at "(ומזה מובן" is not closed in the edition (the Kehot note: "אוצ"ל: וד"ל)"); the ביאור leaves it so, the explanation carries the reader to the end without it.
