# Student read — שער היחוד פרק מו (prompts/student-read.md)

Read 2026-11-22, the built biur.md, by the writer reading as the student.

- "מלך השמיני" stands bare in the text. The gloss says what it is (after the seven kings who reigned and died) and the עץ חיים passage set in below it (שער דרושי הנקודות פ"ג: "עד שבא הדר מלך ח' ואז נתקנו") carries it, so the student is not asked to know Genesis 36 and its reading.
- The Joseph parenthesis is dense: the exile as a dream, the "דורמיטא", the letters of the dream combined anew, "תופר / פורת / פותר", "אברך". The עץ חיים passage on the "דורמיטא" (שער הנסירה פ"א, "ונסתלקו ממנו המוחין הנקרא שינה") anchors the sleep; the verses (בראשית מא, מג; מט, כב) anchor "אברך" and "פורת". "תופר" is glossed by what the text says Joseph does (combines the scattered letters); no more.
- The image of the lamp at noon is given in the Talmud's words (חולין ס, ב, "דשרגא בטיהרא מאי אהני"); the text has "מהני".
- Section 4: the two meanings of "נוגה" are each given their verse; the student can see "הגה מן המסלה" as a pushing out. The point that both are one is the text's (the light in the cloud, the rainbow); the gloss adds only "הבליטה והאור אחד הם", which is the text's "שם בולט והוגה האור כמו יגיה חשכי".
- "כן מראה הנוגה ד כו'": a stray "ד" in the edition's text. The gloss quotes the verse whole (יחזקאל א, כח) and passes over it.
- "ראומה ראו שאני בטל": the student will need the play "ראו מה"; given in the gloss as the text implies ("מה" as בטל), with the sense: a ביטול that shows itself, the felt "יש".
- The זהר בלק passage set in is the one the text cites (33.7 in the export); 33.6 holds "עבד נהורא לנהוריה, לאתלבשא דא בדא", quoted in the gloss for the text's "ועביד נהורא לאתלבשא".
- Section 6: ס"ג above מ"ה, ב"ן rising to ס"ג: the four names were laid out in פרק כז; the student who has them follows. The table's last row sums it.
