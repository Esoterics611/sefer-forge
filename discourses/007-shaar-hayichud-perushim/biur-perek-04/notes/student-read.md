# Student read — שער היחוד פרק ד (prompts/student-read.md)

Read 2026-11-21, the built biur.md, by the writer reading as the student. Fixes applied in biur.src.md, rebuilt.

- Before the read, in writing: "ימות עולם דו"ק" first glossed as זעיר אנפין ונוקבא; read again as "ימות עולם" = ו"ק, the six extremities, the מדות. The footnote on יחודא עילאה replaced by one resting on Tanya's words and the chapter's own ("עד עצמות אור א"ס").
- §5: "לא ראי זה" was attributed to "לשון חז"ל" with a wording ("כראי זה") not checked against a source (the Mishna, בבא קמא א, א, reads "לא הרי"). Applied: plain "לא זו כזו", no attribution.
- §13: "כמ"ש בכתבי'" left unexplained after the Rambam and the Tur, so a student would look for a source in the כתבי האר"י. Applied: "כמו שנכתב בדברי החסידות" ("כתבים" in this קונטרס is דא"ח, as the editor notes in פרק א).
- §15: "הה"מ" glossed "הרב המגיד" only, and "מפיו" left ambiguous (whose mouth). Applied: "רבו, הרב המגיד ממעזריטש", "ומפי אביו שמע זאת המחבר".
- Left: "אורות וכלים" (§4) and "ראשית הקו" (§9) are taken as known to the reader this read assumes, and glossed in a phrase.
- Checked: the first-person "שמעתי" (§15) is the author's, in the text; the build's rule against the first person applies to the explanation only.
