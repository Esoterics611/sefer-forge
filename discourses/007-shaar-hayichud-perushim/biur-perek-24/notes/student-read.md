- The פרק is the נמשל of פרק כג's parable; each step tied back (the soul drawn after pleasure and not pleasure itself; the ניצוח that has no existence without pleasure).
- "(באות י"ב)": the editor reads it as אות י"ט; the explanation points to פרק יט, where the lights of the נאצלים are "גילוי ההעלם", separate from the essence.
- §4: "ז"ת דע"י מלובש בגלגלתא דא"א" set in from Etz Chaim (13.7.1); "המתנשא מימות עולם" read as the text reads it: the seven "days" are clothed, and he is raised above them.
- §4: the wedding parable tied to פרק כא (נקודת הרצון above the hidden reason); the גבורה in the hidden wisdom said in plain words: precision, "this way and not otherwise".
- "אורייתא מחכמה עלאה נפקת" and "אפיק עשר תיקונין..." not quoted beyond the text's own words (their sources are not in sources/).
