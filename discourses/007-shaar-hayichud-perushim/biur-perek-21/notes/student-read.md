# Student read — שער היחוד פרק כא (prompts/student-read.md)

Read 2026-11-21, the built biur.md, by the writer reading as the student.

- The three lines laid out as a table before the text (right, middle, left), each sefira with the text's own word for it, so §§2–3 read against a picture.
- "ענף ... ומובדל הימנה": said in plain words, the חסד is the kindness that branched from reason leaning to kindness, tied back to the "מורכב" of פרק כ.
- §3: "כי יצר לב" tied to פרק כ, where the same verse was given in full.
- §4: "י"ב גבולי אלכסון" given with the words of ספר יצירה ה, א; "בריח התיכון" with its verse, so "מבריח מן הקצה" is read as the text reads it: from end to end, up to the inner כתר.
- §5: "אין טעם לרצון" tied to פרק יז; "שתוק כך עלה במח'" given with the Gemara (מנחות כט, ב), so a student sees what "a will with no reason" means.
- §6: "מלפניך, מלפני הפרסא" said to be the text's reading of the words of the prayer.
