# Student read — שער היחוד פרק מ (prompts/student-read.md)

Read 2026-11-22, the built biur.md, by the writer reading as the student.

- "אית מח' ואית מח'": the two thoughts laid out side by side in the levels table (what, where, letters, above) before the text.
- "הרצון נק' ג"כ בשם מח'": shown from Scripture with ירמיה יח, ח ("אשר חשבתי לעשות"), the verse the editor points to for "כאשר חשב".
- "גידי המוח... נערוי"ן" glossed as the nerves, and the text's own example (stretching and bending a limb at once by will) kept, since it carries the point: will without letters.
- The Yiddish "פאר שטאנד" and "דער פאר נעמען אין פאר שטאנד אליין" glossed in Hebrew (understanding; the grasp in understanding alone).
- "מי דלא קיימא לשאלה": the Zohar (introduction) given in a footnote; there "מי" is what stands to be asked, and above it there is no question; the gloss follows the text's wording.
- Checked: "רצון לרצון" to פרק יח; "רצון קדום" to פרק יז; the essence's will to פרק י; "מלכות דתבונה... בענין הנבדל" to פרק ב; "לאה" to פרק לז; "חש"ב מ"ה" and "כח מה" to פרק כה; "טעם כמוס לרצון" to פרק כא; letters in the essence of the soul to פרק לח.
