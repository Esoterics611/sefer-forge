# Student read — שער היחוד פרק כז (prompts/student-read.md)

Read 2026-11-21, the built biur.md, by the writer reading as the student.

- "איהו וחיוהי חד... איהו וגרמוהי חד": both phrases translated in place and tied to lights and vessels; Etz Chaim 42.5.1 in a footnote quotes the same Tikkunim words.
- The four names given as the four fillings of the Name, each with its sefira, so "ע"ב ס"ג מ"ה ב"ן" is not left as letters.
- "תלת שליטין" set in from the Zohar (תרומה), so מוחא, לבא and כבדא are seen in their source before the text maps them to נשמה, רוח and נפש.
- רמ"ח: the arithmetic spelled out (9×9 = 81, ×3 = 243, +5 = 248).
- §4: the two things a vessel does (changes the essence; limits) are numbered in the text ("הא'", "והב'") and kept as two paragraphs.
- §5: the point a student might miss, that matter is "from the end of" the form itself, said in plain words before the examples.
