# Student read — שער היחוד פרק מה (prompts/student-read.md)

Read 2026-11-22, the built biur.md, by the writer reading as the student.

- The opening verse is quoted in the text without "אלהים"; the gloss gives the verse whole, so the student sees it.
- "אור באלף" without its source would puzzle; the gloss gives בראשית רבה's words, "בתורתו של רבי מאיר מצאו כתוב כתנות אור". "אחר הבירורים דרפ"ח" is left as the text gives it, glossed as the 288 sparks; the student who has not met the term will need it from elsewhere. Left, as the chapter does not explain it.
- The shattering is introduced here for the first time in שער היחוד (grep: no "שבירה" in פרקים א–מד). The עץ חיים passage (שער התיקון פ"ה, "העצמות היה שלם והכלים קטנים ולכן מתו") gives the student the doctrine in its own words before the parable.
- "בל"א פאר נעמט": Yiddish; glossed by what the sentence says (the vessel grasps and holds the light), not by a dictionary.
- The parable of "ויאמר" losing its letters: the student may ask why "וי"ו מם"; the text means only that the letters stand scattered. The gloss says no more.
- The dream (ברכות נה, ב) is given in the Talmud's words, both halves: "אין מראין לו לאדם אלא מהרהורי לבו" and the elephant through the needle's eye. With them the point (even the strange thing keeps its root) is clear; that sentence of the gloss is drawn from the text's "רק מעין הרהורי לבו דוקא".
- "וכמשי"ת" on Joseph the interpreter of dreams: glossed "כמו שיתבאר בפרק מו"; the editor's notes for (מו) list "יוסף פותר חלומות", so the pointer holds.
- Section 5: the claim "the farthest parable holds the deepest source" is the text's; the זהר תרומה passage set in says only that every word had three thousand parables. The gloss keeps the claim on the text, "כמ"ש בזהר", and does not stretch the זהר.
- "אימא פרישת גדפהא", "בשלום אותיות מלבוש", "סכך דסוכה": three pointers "במ"א". Glossed only by their plain sense (the wings spread, the letters of בשלום and מלבוש are the same five, the covering of the סוכה), and joined by the one thing the text says of all three, the guarding מקיף.
