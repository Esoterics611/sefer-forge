## Understanding and Lower Understanding

> Chapter two. Now, it is known that within Understanding itself there are two levels: Understanding, and lower Understanding. Lower Understanding is the spreading out of the light of Understanding.

That is: we spoke before of contemplation. The whole treatise is the Treatise on Contemplation, which is the matter of Understanding; and we said that contemplation is the study within Understanding, the fiftieth gate, through which we reach the depth of the idea. And what we say here of Understanding in a person's mind is, of course, parallel to Understanding among the divine attributes; there is a parallel here between the two systems.

Now the Rebbe explains a new word to us: lower Understanding. In Hebrew, its name is close to the modern word for insight. It is the outcome, the practical result of Understanding. So the inwardness of Understanding, which we said is the fiftieth gate, is the depth of the idea, from which all the length and breadth flow, and they are the river, which corresponds to Understanding. And this is the world of thought that flows and wells up from the spring outward, in length and breadth, as we explained in chapter one. And the matter of lower Understanding is the use of Understanding and of the river to bring about insight: to arrive at a new idea from the understanding we have received. In the words of our Sages, of blessed memory: "one who understands one thing from another." Understanding one thing from another is lower Understanding.

## The Lower Father and Mother

Now let us see how the Rebbe explains this, and through it explains the ideas of the holy Zohar:

> As is written in the Zohar: the lower Father and Mother are Israel the Elder and lower Understanding, and they are called the lower Mother, and so on.

That is: the Rebbe brings us from the Zohar the meaning of lower Understanding: that the lower Father and Mother are Israel the Elder and lower Understanding. It is known that the configurations of the Father and the Mother correspond to Wisdom and Understanding; only, in a configuration, each attribute, each point, is filled out with its own details. So when we speak of Wisdom as such, as in "wisdom is found from nothing," we use the name of the attribute, Wisdom. When we speak of structures, so to speak, within the attribute of Wisdom, we make it into the configuration of the Father; and then we have the Kingship of Wisdom and the Foundation of Wisdom. The Foundation of Wisdom, the Foundation of the Father, is for example the root of Moses our teacher. So the Father and the Mother are configurations corresponding to the attributes of Wisdom and Understanding, and each configuration is a whole structure, so that we can speak of the details of that configuration.

And here the Zohar explains further: corresponding to the upper Father and Mother, which are Wisdom and Understanding, there are the lower Father and Mother. There is a kind of lower Wisdom and lower Understanding, and these are Israel the Elder and lower Understanding. So just as lower Understanding is the outcome, the result, of Understanding, so the configuration of Israel the Elder is the result of Wisdom. We will see these configurations as they are within the structures of the world of Emanation, of the Small Countenance and the rest. But for now it is enough to understand that just as Understanding has lower Understanding, its outcome, so Wisdom too has its outcome.

The configuration of Israel the Elder is the spark the Zohar speaks of, for example, as a point within its palace, the palace guarding the point. So the point of an idea also has its palace, so to speak, which is the outcome of the idea: that is Israel the Elder. And again, we will come back to explain Israel the Elder later in the discourse. But the point of Israel the Elder and lower Understanding is this: just as the Father and the Mother, Wisdom and Understanding, unite together to bring forth Knowledge, so Israel the Elder and lower Understanding unite to give birth to our insights. Now, in any case: just as you have the Mother, which is the attribute of Understanding, as we said, the river of thought in which the inner point, Wisdom, is revealed, so in lower Understanding the point of Israel the Elder is revealed, which is lower Wisdom.

## Deep Waters, and a Man of Understanding Draws Them Out

The Rebbe continues:

> Lower Understanding is the spreading out of the light of Understanding, because the vessel that receives the grasp of Understanding has taken it in well. So it spreads out in it, to bring it into several matters set apart from the grasp itself; and this is called a man of understanding.

Let us make it clear: lower Understanding is the outcome, as we said before. The Rebbe says that to bring about matters set apart from the grasp itself is to understand one thing from another, and this is called a man of understanding. The Rebbe continues:

> As in "Deep waters; and a man of understanding will draw them out." Deep waters are the depth of Understanding that remains hidden in its essence; and precisely a man of understanding draws them out and brings them up from the hiddenness, to spread them out in several different ways, to increase those who receive, to water the flock.

Notice that the whole parable of the water goes on here. This is the point: the deep waters of contemplation, of the fiftieth gate, of the inwardness of Understanding, need a man of understanding. A man of understanding brings out the outcome from these deep waters, to water the flock. And here we see that all the Sages and all the prophets, all their matters, of the mind and of insights in Torah, down to the insights of the human mind and all the ideas and insights in the world, come from "the river that goes out of Eden to water the garden." And the man of understanding, who is one who engages in the work of contemplation, draws up these deep waters, and from there brings one thing out of another, until he draws them out and brings them up from the hiddenness, to spread them out in several different ways. And this is the work that comes to contemplation.

The Rebbe continues:

> And this is as we see plainly: when an idea and a reasoning are too deep for a person's grasp to hold, they are called deep waters, which is the depth of the idea described above, from which the length and the breadth come. And precisely a man of understanding draws them out. That is, he brings to light the hidden depth of the idea, until a person's grasp can hold it, as if it were not deep at all; like one who draws up deep waters and brings the depth near, and so on.

That is: the matter of a man of understanding is to draw from the deep waters, from the spring of the mind, through contemplation and understanding of the root of the matter, in length and breadth, until he brings the outcome up from there, so that he can clothe it in an idea without depth, an intention without depth, which brings the depth near. What was deep and needed much study to grasp, the man of understanding draws up, the idea, the water, until he brings it to the surface.

## The Power of Lower Understanding: from Hidden to Revealed

> Accordingly, it must be said that the power of lower Understanding is the power that spreads out from the power of Understanding, to bring its depth down into revelation, to those who receive.

That is: the Rebbe says that this is the power of lower Understanding: it spreads out from Understanding, to break open the depth into a revealed flowing that reaches the surface, from where that idea can be received. That is lower Understanding.

> Which is not so for one who is not a man of understanding: even when he has a grasp in the mind, in Understanding, what the world calls a quick grasper, his grasp may stay only as it is in itself, in the mind of his understanding, which is called the spirit of his understanding alone; and nothing will spread out from it below, into any matter set apart from the grasp itself.

So lower Understanding is a power. Just as we said that study, called the inwardness of Understanding, the fiftieth gate, is the power to dig and to dive deep into the depth of the idea, there is a power called the power of lower Understanding, which gives the power to draw up from the depth of the idea to the surface, and to increase those who receive the wisdom, without their needing to dive into the depth.

> And even though the grasp has length, breadth and depth, as said above, all of it is only within the grasp itself as it is; it has not been revealed below, in a matter set apart. And all the more so the depth of the idea: it stays in its depth, hidden within itself.

That means: even when things are revealed, through the man of understanding, the depth of the idea, without the power of lower Understanding, stays hidden. So the power of lower Understanding brings it out of hiddenness into revelation.

> And so too the length and the breadth: all of it within the grasp itself, as it is in its own place alone. As an example of all this, we see the depth of dialectic in the Talmud, with its reasons in many arguments, this way and that: even though they come into the grasp of Understanding, they still cannot bring out of the idea itself any ruling at all, liable or exempt. That is, he cannot bring out of the reasoning any liability according to this idea, nor any exemption according to this idea; for the idea and the reasoning are still only an intellectual grasp, and nothing spreads out from it into a matter set apart from it, which is to hold liable or to exempt.

That is: in the way of studying the revealed Torah too, one who has not reached the depth of the idea cannot learn from it and draw a conclusion. The whole study of the revealed Torah is to teach a person how to reach a conclusion and to rule on the law. To repeat all the arguments is not the completeness of Understanding. The completeness of Understanding, up to lower Understanding, is that from the arguments you have learned you can bring to light a new idea, which the Kabbalists call the light of offspring. And all these words here explain what is within the attribute of Understanding. And lower Understanding, which is also called the Kingship of Understanding, and these are the ideas explained here, is in all the writings of Kabbalah. One can understand one thing from another, what these words mean. So the meaning of lower Understanding is the power of the outcome: to understand, and to decide an insight, which is a new idea, from the understanding you have received and understood.

## Formless Matter: from Potential to Actual

The Rebbe continues:

> And it is like formless matter, of which it is not yet known what will come of it, or what will be done with it.

Here he goes on: formless matter is a power in potential that has not yet come into revelation, or matter that has not yet put on a form. So when there is some understanding without lower Understanding, it is still in potential; and the matter of the power of lower Understanding is to bring it from potential into actuality. The Rebbe continues:

> But the power of lower Understanding, which spreads out from the mind of Understanding, is what is able to bring the grasp itself to spread out below it, into every matter set apart from it: either to liability or to exemption, or to be stirred in the heart, and also to bring it into thought in several different ways.

That is: this power of lower Understanding also brings to a ruling: a person can think and draw a conclusion from the knowledge. But it is also what brings the heart to be stirred. When you understand the depth of the idea, and know how to clothe it and draw conclusions from it, the depth of the idea also opens for you in the heart; and that is Knowledge. Knowledge is the revelation in the heart, the inwardness of the emotions: by understanding a Godly idea thoroughly through contemplation, until you know how to draw conclusions from it, the conclusions themselves rule over thought, speech and action.

## The Seven Lower Attributes of Understanding: Emotions Within the Mind

The Rebbe continues:

> But we see that within Understanding itself there are emotions, called the seven lower attributes of Understanding, and called the emotions within the mind: an inclination of the mind toward exemption, or an inclination of the mind toward liability, as is known. That is, the emotions within the mind are still hidden within the grasp itself.

Let us explain. That is: just as we said that lower Understanding is the Kingship of Understanding, which is the practical result, the learning of the outcome from what you have understood, so there are all the seven lower attributes. Among the ten divine attributes we have the first three, and then the seven lower ones. The powers of the mind are the inwardness of every configuration, which can be understood as the configuration relates to itself. As, by way of parable, in a person: there are his three powers of the mind; that is his inner personality, which is his essence, and he does not reveal it to others, nor even to himself. And there are the seven lower attributes, which are Kindness, Might, Beauty, Victory, Splendor, Foundation and Kingship. They are revealed, so to speak; revealed to himself; and these are the emotions. And there is Kingship, which is how he is revealed to others.

So there are three stages here in general. One is the powers of the mind: the inwardness and essence of the person. There are the emotions, corresponding to the configuration of the Small Countenance, the six attributes from Kindness to Foundation; these are the emotions, the person himself, not only the personality: how he understands himself and knows himself, but does not yet reveal to others. And Kingship is how he reveals himself to others. So it is always a matter of the seven lower and the first three. So in the attribute of Understanding too there are the first three of Understanding, which we said correspond to contemplation: to dig into the depth of the fiftieth gate, to reach the inwardness of Understanding and its bond with Wisdom. And there is the entirely other side, the Kingship of lower Understanding, where there is the influence outward.

And now we have defined a new idea, the seven lower attributes of Understanding: these are the levels of Understanding before a new idea is born from it, but where there is already a certain being. How do I think about it? What do I think about it? Wisdom is only a spark of thought, not clothed in letters at all. In Understanding it already begins to be clothed in letters. But in the first three, in the inwardness of Understanding, in contemplation, there is no "how I feel," "how I connect to the matter." There is only the matter as it is. When we study a deep idea, there is no more room; feeling is completely set aside, until I do not relate to the material personally: I understand the idea as it is. But then, in Understanding, before it comes down to the Kingship of Understanding, to lower Understanding, from where it gives birth to new ideas, there are the emotions of Understanding, the seven lower attributes of Understanding, in which is how I relate to this idea. There begins the matter of what I think about it, how I feel with it. It is still an inner matter, and it affects the revelation of this idea onward. So this is what we said.

## The Kingship of Lower Understanding: the Letters of Thought

> That is, he is also able to bring the depth of Understanding into revelation, as said above; and this is from the lower Father and Mother, Israel the Elder and lower Understanding. Israel the Elder are the emotions of Wisdom, and lower Understanding the emotions of Understanding. But they come into revelation outward, and as the Kingship of lower Understanding, which is the idea coming into thought, called the letters of thought.

An explanation. Now let us explain what Israel the Elder and lower Understanding are. Israel the Elder is the emotions within Wisdom: within the idea there is still somehow how I relate to it, but it is subconscious. And lower Understanding is already the emotions within Understanding, which are revealed, in the mind: how I relate to it, how I feel, how I feel with it. Here the idea already comes into awareness. Israel the Elder and lower Understanding are called the lower Father and Mother, which are the powers of the mind that belong to the emotions, in the language of Hasidic teaching. That means it is already an idea I can relate to. Although, again, as long as it has not reached lower Understanding itself, the Kingship of Understanding, I do not feel myself within the idea; but in the subconscious there is already a feeling; there is a belonging to the idea. That is, in the upper Father and Mother, Wisdom and Understanding themselves, there is no belonging to the emotions at all. An absolute idea, which has nothing to do with how I feel about it. But when this idea comes down to clothe itself in my mind, in my personality, now there is how I feel with it. All of this is a parable.

The Rebbe continues:

> And from the seven emotions of Understanding they come into revelation outward, and as the Kingship of lower Understanding, which is the idea coming into thought, called the letters of thought, in which the idea is clothed. It is an outer vessel for studious thought, which is the revelation of the grasp of Understanding itself, from the idea and the reasoning themselves. And from the Kingship of lower Understanding is drawn the root of the letters of thought themselves, which is called Leah; and it is the enlarged last letter of the word "One."

Now, what we said of the Kingship of lower Understanding: first of all, I explained it imprecisely before. Lower Understanding is in fact the emotions within Understanding, and the Kingship of lower Understanding is already the letters. So when we have the letters of the mind, when a person thinks, there are letters in the thought. These letters have already come out of the Kingship of lower Understanding; as we know, "Kingship is the mouth; we call it the Oral Torah," in the Tikkunei Zohar: Kingship is always the letters in the mouth. So in thought too there is, as it were, a mouth in the thought, from which the letters of thought come out: that is the Kingship of lower Understanding.

The Rebbe explains what this is, as the enlarged last letter of the word "One." In the contemplation of "Hear, O Israel," we also see the enlarged last letter of the word "Hear," which is the seven emotions as they are included in the Kingship of lower Understanding; and the enlarged letter of "One" is the Kingship of lower Understanding too. Excuse me: lower Understanding is the enlarged letter of "Hear," and the enlarged letter of "One" is the Kingship of lower Understanding. All of these are intentions in the Gate of Intentions, in Fruit of the Tree of Life. But now we have some grasp of what lower Understanding is and what the Kingship of lower Understanding is. So he hints to us, so that you know: in the books of Kabbalah, the enlarged last letter of "One" hints at the Kingship of lower Understanding. So you have a certain contemplation, which is the letters of thought.

## Son and Daughter: Giving Birth to the Emotions

The Rebbe continues:

> And this is why lower Understanding is called son and daughter, as is known; for lower Understanding too has three things: depth, length and breadth. The depth is the very power of this lower Understanding within that grasp, as it is when it takes it in; and in it the depth of the grasp described above can come entirely into revelation, as we said. The length is its descent into another matter, set apart, to teach by it and to do many deeds through it. And the breadth is the spreading out of lower Understanding within the grasp itself, putting it together in thought in many different ways, not only as it is.

As we explained the depth, breadth and length in the idea itself, also in the inwardness, in the spring and the river described above, so in thought that is revealed, or flowing, there are these three aspects too: depth, length and breadth.

So why is lower Understanding called son and daughter? The Rebbe continues:

> That lower Understanding includes son and daughter means that precisely from lower Understanding the emotions are born, revealed as something in their own right. For from Understanding itself the emotions would not be born, even though it is called the mother of the children; but it is the upper Mother. Whereas lower Understanding is called the lower Mother, to give birth to the emotions; and this is son and daughter, love and awe, which are included precisely in lower Understanding, the spreading out of the light of Understanding below, as said above.

That is: son and daughter hint here at the Small Countenance and its feminine counterpart. This too is brought in the book of the Tanya, and they correspond to love and awe. And this is already in the emotions, already in the offspring, the result, of lower Understanding. So indeed lower Understanding itself gives birth to the emotions, how I relate to it; and the emotions are love and awe, the Small Countenance and its feminine counterpart, which is son and daughter. The Rebbe continues:

> Indeed, "by understanding it is established," which is the existence of the emotions, the powers of the mind: this is the lower Understanding within them. For lower Understanding is like an intermediary, to bring the powers of the mind, as the emotions of Understanding, into the emotions of the heart; which is like the power of bringing into a matter set apart, which is the main matter of lower Understanding, as said above.

So again: the matter of lower Understanding is to give birth to the emotions of the heart, and down to thought, which is the Kingship of lower Understanding itself, in the letters of speech. And all these layers come down to how to reveal the depth you understood in the inwardness: how it is revealed, to give birth. And so all the worlds are born, in the configurations of the world of Emanation, where everything is ideas and higher understanding; only the parable is not at all like what it stands for: our physical thought is only there to give us some parable, to understand how thought is above.

## The Bent Letter and the Straight: Conclusion

The Rebbe concludes:

> It is known also that in Understanding there is the letter of fifty bent and the letter of fifty straight; the bent letter is the breadth, and the straight letter is the length.

Yes: the letter whose number is fifty has two forms. The bent form, which sits low, and the straight form at the end of a word, which goes down vertically.

> When its name is spelled out, the letter of fifty is completed with the straight one; which is not so in the spelling out of other letters, for the length is included in the breadth. So it is in lower Understanding. And indeed the doubled letter of fifty is in the matter called contemplation.

The word for contemplation, too, has the letter of fifty twice.

> Which is the inwardness of Understanding, called study.

To teach you that the inwardness of Understanding, called study, is what is drawn down in the final, straight letter, to give birth from it to the emotions. Yes, the Rebbe continues:

> This is the inwardness of Understanding, called study, to stand upon a matter, which is called the Understanding within Understanding. It is an intermediary between Understanding and lower Understanding, by way of the straight letter of fifty, because of which it is called contemplation.

And blessed be God forever, amen and amen.
