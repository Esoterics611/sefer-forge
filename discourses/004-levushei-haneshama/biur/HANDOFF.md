### What sections 11–19 say (short)
11. The garments are one thing with the צלם of section 2: "צלם דאימא" (לבוש נה"י דאימא, "חותם"), inner
    (clothes the נר"ן) and surrounding (= חשמל). "הבל דגרמי" names both: "שניהם אור א'". שער ל"ט דרוש י"ב:
    the garment from אימא is the חותם, making it is the raising of מ"ן, and it is "גדול" than what it clothes.
12. One skin, two names: with the light of תבונה on it, חשמל (the good in נוגה); without it, נוגה. All repair
    = repelling the חיצונים by אור הבינה, inside and outside. "וקרוב לומר": inside by mitzvot of speech,
    outside by practical ones.
13. The garment is called reward because it is drawn from אימא = עוה"ב; but it is not עוה"ב, it is what lets
    the soul bear and enjoy her light. No ascent without an upper garment; a lacking garment = not wholly
    clarified, hence גיהנם first. The Saba's garments are for descending below one's level.
14. גן עדן העליון is of בריאה; the mitzvot work a זיווג in אצילות, rooted in מו"ס דא"א. Even a נפש דעשיה:
    its מ"ן rise to מלכות דאצילות. No בירור but in אצילות.
15. From practical mitzvot the garment is only for נפש דעשיה, yet the זיווג is in אצילות, because on weekdays
    the vessels of זו"נ דאצילות are clothed in בי"ע. The parenthesis: the descent (a) gives the נפש power to
    raise from the kelipot, (b) itself raises the "אלקות שבנוגה", with the mitzvot's help.
16. "ציצית ביצירה, תפילין בבריאה" = where the חיצונים are repelled, by light drawn from אצילות (from
    "עשיה שבאצילות", its חיצוניות); like ק"ש: a זיווג of או"א דאצילות, drawn into בריאה.
17. The Names intended in ברכות, מקרא, משנה, תלמוד are all of אצילות.
18. All parts of Torah are in אצילות; they differ in how far each descends and is clothed. "אי נמי אצילות
    ממש שבעשיה" is rejected by "אך מאחר": שם מ"ה is of an inner vessel, and inner vessels are not clothed in
    עשיה (ע"ח 44.5.8).
19. The close: "Torah itself is in יצירה" (49.5.1) = the level of רוח; Talmud is רוח שבבריאה, Mishnah רוח
    שברוח, kavanah the נשמה of both. 49.5.2 proves Talmud is in the upper בריאה, but speaks of one whose
    soul is of בריאה; perhaps for a נפש דעשיה it is "בריאה שבעשיה" (צ"ע). "אך זה אינו" rejects that: souls
    differ only in kavanah, not in the plain study itself.

## What the reading has established (short)
1. Two בירורים. Souls are clarified by rising from the kelipot to be מ"נ in נוקבא דז"א, before birth, by others'
   prayers and mitzvot. The soul's descent into a body is "דוגמת גלות השכינה": only to clarify its own garments,
   by Torah and practical mitzvot; that is why mitzvot are "חיוב יותר" than prayer (prayer דרבנן; garments
   cannot be repaired by others). The Tzemach Tzedek says exactly this ("כל נשמה הבאה לעוה"ז כבר ניתקנה קודם
   בואה... וירידתה לעוה"ז הוא כמו גלות השכינה לברר לבושים"), and sums up three kinds: (א) מ"ן עצמן דנוקבא =
   souls not included in Adam: by prayer only; (ב) souls included in Adam: by prayers and also by mitzvot (prayer
   in פנימיות); (ג) every person's garments: only by Torah and mitzvot.
2. Clarification is by מ"ד (the male); garments are clarified by מ"ד דז"א = the ה' חסדים (מ"ע).
3. The garments = צלם דאימא, inner and surrounding (חשמל), clarified from קליפת נוגה.
4. "והשתא א"ש": the garment comes from אימא (עוה"ב) but is not עוה"ב; it lets the soul bear the light.
5. "יפה שעה אחת": גן עדן is of בריאה; mitzvot work a זיווג in אצילות (מו"ס דא"א, י"ג חיוורתא); proofs from the
   weekday descent of זו"נ, ציצית/תפילין/נט"י, the names intended in blessings and in מקרא–משנה–תלמוד.
The Alter Rebbe's own parallels: תניא, קונטרס אחרון essays 2 and 4 (`sources/md/tanya/05-…`).

## Settled since `open-questions.md` was written (fold these in)
- "חיוב יותר" = Torah and mitzvot are more obligatory than prayer (צ"צ, explicitly).

  **כאכילת העכו"ם**" (dropped in print by the censor — the edition's note).

  ש"א "פ"ו" is "פ"ז"; טעמי המצוות, סוף ההקדמה = "ד"ה סוד מציאות המצות כולם".
