## a. What this part teaches

The gloss: what Etz Chaim gate 39 says, that the נר"ן are refined through
prayers, means their rising out of the kelipot to become מ"ן in the Nukva of
Z"A. Etz Chaim 39:1 brings the Zohar (Pekudei): when the exile ends, at "the
feet", Mashiach comes. The fallen kings of Tohu are one Adam, head to feet, sunk
in the kelipot. At every union above we raise, by our prayer, the holy sparks in
it as מ"ן. When the sparks of its feet are raised too, the whole Adam is
repaired, and Mashiach comes.

The speaker reads it through the parts before. The whole exile is a refining of
fallen sparks. In the person, each choice of good leaves its mark on the צלם,
the garments of consciousness and personality; through Torah, mitzvot and trials
he refines the sparks of his garments. So in the world, which is one great
Adam, as man is a small world. The Shechinah goes into exile to refine sparks,
as the נר"ן go down into the body. The concealment that makes free choice
possible is where Torah and mitzvot work. The world too has a צלם, the
garments of its collective soul. "המלכים" are the lights of Tohu, the root of
this world's longings. History is the collective refining, as choice is the
individual's.

His main point, from the passage: not the mitzvot alone, prayer too refines
(the user, 2026-11-09: "also through Tefilah you have refining of the
garments"), and this is called העלאת מ"ן. In the world as a whole, prayers
refine its sparks until the last, lowest ones, "the feet", and then Mashiach.
Humanity advances toward it, as the individual advances toward the revelation of
his own Mashiach, רוח הקודש.

Then he explains what העלאת מ"ן is. It is the arousal from below: choosing good
in a trial, as in the example of tzedakah given against one's will, for the
mitzvah's sake, with love of one's fellow, compassion and prayer. That good
marks the world, refines the garments and the habits, down to the body. In the
union, מ"ן is the female's arousal before she receives. The female is this
world, the male is the Holy One, blessed be He, and their union is the divine
unity revealed. In exile the female has no arousal and nothing is bestowed: the
exile of the Shechinah. Its repair is the arousal from below, by prayer or by
mitzvot, which draws from above מיין דכורין in proportion. In exile this is
hidden, so that choice remains, but in prayer it is seen. So the redemption
waits on people whose soul shines in prayer, through teshuvah. The world is
ready, and the individual's arousal arouses the soul from above, until the
Shechinah leaves its exile.

   refined, "בעלייתן מן הקליפות להיות מ"נ". Its continuation (box 4's stretch, not
   printed here) sets against it their descent into the body for the practical
   mitzvot, "לברר ולתקן הלבושים שלהם". The speaker (2026-11-09): through prayer too
   the garments are refined, and this is העלאת מ"ן. The text says it in his words
   ("כמו המצוות, גם התפילה מבררת"), quoting the gloss for what prayer refines.
   For the speaker: should the distinction the gloss draws next be met here, or
   in הערה ד'?
