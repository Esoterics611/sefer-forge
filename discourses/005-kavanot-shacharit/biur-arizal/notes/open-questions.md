# open-questions

What stays open after part 1 (מבוא, §1–§4), 2026-11-12. Not printed.

## Where the texts themselves differ (stated in the explanation, not resolved)

1. **Where בריאה ends.** SK Prayers.1.2: בריאה runs to the end of ברכת אבות, which is "ראש
   הבריאה הנקרא היכל ק"ק דבריאה". SK Prayers.1.3 and PEC Prayer.6.4: ה' שפתי תפתח is היכל ק"ק
   דבריאה, and ע"ב is intended from ברכת אבות. Both are given in the מבוא. §9 (part 3) must
   settle what ברכת אבות is, from the דרושי העמידה.
2. **The point of Rachel in the morning.** SK Tefillin.5.6–5.9: her keter-point rises to
   אצילות at dawn and stands פב"פ at ז"א's left arm from the hand tefillin on; the other nine
   are in בי"ע and come up at ברכת אבות. SK Prayers.2.2 (quoted in the מבוא) and "ביאור ברכת
   אבות דר"ה": the point stands in היכל ק"ק דבריאה and rises in ק"ש / ברכת אבות, אב"א. The
   דרוש flags the contradiction twice ("וצ"ע שם"; "נראה בהפך ממ"ש כאן") and refers to
   "הקדמת ברכת אבות דשחרית דחול" for his view. §3 states both; §8–§9 must read that הקדמה.
   The Rashash's level may turn on this.
3. **טלית קטן: inner or outer of יצירה.** SK Prayers.3.3 and PEC Fringes.1.11: the small tallit
   is the פנימיות of ג"ת דיצירה, the large the חיצוניות. SK Tzitzit.6.3 cites the same place as
   saying "ציצית קטן הוא בחיצו' היצירה ולא בפנימיות". Possibly "חיצוניות" there is חיצוניות
   העולמות (all four deeds are in it, 2.2), within which 3.3 distinguishes inner and outer; or
   a slip of the text. §2 follows 3.3 and quotes 6.3 from the words after that phrase.
4. **What the large tallit is.** SK Tzitzit.1.1: the מקיף of ז"א "כשהגדיל ונעשו לו י"ס
   גמורות"; 5.2: "טלית גדול כנגד יניקה", with no tallit for גדלות (that is tefillin); 6.3 and
   PEC Fringes.1.13: "יצירה דגדלות ממש". §2 sets them side by side.
5. **ציצית: עולמות or נשמות.** SK Tzitzit.5.2: ציצית is against "העולמות החיצונית", tefillin
   against the מקיף of the נשמות. SK Prayers.2.2: all four deeds fix the חיצוניות of the
   worlds. §2 quotes 5.2 as it is. Not reconciled.
6. **The eighteen blessings.** SK (Prayers.1.2; Morning Blessings.7, .9) and PEC Prayer.6.14:
   eighteen from ענט"י to ברכת התורה. PEC Blessings.5.1, 7.2: the heading says twenty; three
   (ענט"י, אשר יצר, אלהי נשמה = א"א, אבא, אימא דעשיה) and eighteen from הנותן לשכוי (= ז"א
   דעשיה): twenty-one by that count. SK Morning Blessings.13–15 has the same three partzufim.
   Which blessings make the eighteen in each count is not worked out.
7. **ישתבח.** SK Prayers.1.2: its thirteen praises are at the end of יצירה; 3.9: "בבריאה יש
   י"ג שבחים שיש בישתבח". For §6–§7 (part 2).
8. **Do the outer worlds rise.** SK Prayers.2.2: the חיצוניות is "נכלל" and "עולים ונכללים"
   with the souls; 1.3 and 3.5: the worlds do not rise, they stay and become inner. PEC
   Prayer.6.9–6.12 gives four hearings (weekdays: only the inner; Shabbat: also the outer / in
   exile not even on Shabbat). The מבוא reads 2.2 by 3.5. PEC Prayer.7.53 (weekday/Shabbat by
   חיצוניות הבריאה) was read but not used.
9. **The eleven spices.** Ten sparks + one מקיף (SK 3.12; PEC World of Action.4.3, by its index) or the ten
   sefirot of עשיה + the one holy force (SK 3.8; PEC 4.2, "תחילה שמעתי"). Both given.
10. **The thirteen levels of עשיה.** Ten restored + the three that rose (3.9), or thirteen in
    עשיה alone (the "נלע"ד ששמעתי" there). Both given.

## Not read, or not used, in part 1

- PEC Gate of Prayer ch. 8 ("כוונה אחרת בענין עליית העולמות בחול", with נתן's "צ"ע" in 8.2):
  another account of what the four deeds make (מוחין of נפש of each world). Its index says it
  differs from the opening of the gate. **Worth reading by whoever checks the מבוא.**
- PEC Gate of Prayer ch. 4–5 (the parallel of SK דרושים ב–ג): by index only.
- PEC Gate of Prayer.7.57–7.63 and Blessings.7.17–7.22 ("ואלה מוסיף על הראשונים"): a short
  account of the whole morning by the מלכות's ascent (סנדלפון, מטטרון). Read 7.57 only.
- The ק"ש said with the קרבנות (PEC World of Action.2.8–2.9), פרשת העקידה, and "מה אנו מה
  חיינו" (2.7): not in SK's stretch for stage 04; not explained in §4.
- The plague yichudim (SK Prayers.3.14–3.18): read; left out of §4 as outside the basics,
  except that the incense stops the plague.
- SK Tefillin דרושים א–ב (the scribe's kavanot, the shapes of the letters), ד (the two shins,
  368), the first and third accounts of Rabbenu Tam in 6.2: read; only what §3 needs is used.
- SK Tzitzit דרוש ג (the sixteen מוחין of אבא and of אימא, their names and sums): read; §2
  uses only that the fringes are their light as מקיף. §8 (ק"ש) will need it.
- SK Intentions of Blessings (ברוך, אתה, בית הכנסת, the שם מ"ב by the days): read; nothing of
  it is in part 1. The general kavana of a blessing (שביל, נתיב, רל"ב) belongs wherever §1 is
  widened, or in §9; the synagogue kavanot (ברגש = אבגית"ץ, the seven הקפות) have no section
  of their own in the skeleton. Ronnie to decide whether they enter §4's opening.
- SK Morning Blessings.26 (the hundred blessings: ס"ג + ל"ז): read; not used.

## After parts 2–3 (2026-11-13)

- **1 (where בריאה ends): answered in §9** by SK Standing Prayer.1.1.3 — the verse is still
  בריאה; ברוך of ברכת אבות is היכל ק"ק דבריאה included in מלכות דאצילות and rising with her.
- **2 (Rachel's point): still open**, now stated twice (§3, §8–§9). SK Standing Prayer.2.1.3
  ("ספי' הכתר שבה נשארת באצילות תמיד") agrees with the tefillin account; SK Prayers.2.2 and the
  "ברכת אבות דר"ה" do not. The "הקדמת ברכת אבות דשחרית דחול" was not read.
- **7 (ישתבח, יצירה or בריאה):** §6 follows Morning Prayers.26 (יצירה, its ג"ר risen); the
  "בבריאה" of Prayers.3.9 is quoted in §4 as it stands. Not reconciled.
- **Where the union of Shacharit is made.** SK Prayers.1.3: "בעת שמתפללין תפלת י"ח בעמידה
  ואז נעשה שם הזווג העליון"; SK Shema 3.1.6, 4.1.2 and Falling on the Face 2.2: the union of
  Yaakov and Rachel is at נפילת אפים, the drop beginning at שים שלום. §9 and §11–§12 say both.
- **Part 3 (§9–§13) was written from selected passages**, not from a full reading of the gates
  (שער הכוונות for stages 09–13 is about 300K characters). Read in full: SK Standing Prayer
  1.1.3, 2.1.3, 2.2.1, 2.6.1, 2.6.2 (part), 2.6.4, 2.6.5, 6.1.2, 6.1.3, 6.1.21, 6.1.27;
  Repetition 1.1.3, 5.1.3; PEC Repetition 1.2; Vaya'avor 1.3; PEC Confession 1.3, 8.33;
  Falling on the Face 2.2, 2.3; Torah Reading 1.3, 1.4; Ashrei 1.2; PEC Reading the Torah 6.1.
  Not read: the detailed kavanot of ברכת אבות (2.3–2.5, דרושים ג–ה), the middle blessings one
  by one (6.1.4–6.1.20), the קדושה of the repetition (Repetition 2–4), the 13 תיקוני דיקנא in
  detail (Vaya'avor 2–7), נ"א דרושים 3–5, the kavanot of אשרי / ובא לציון / יענך. A fuller
  level-1 treatment of §9 especially would use them.
