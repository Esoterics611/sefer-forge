# The independent check of part 1 (מבוא, §1–§4), 2026-11-13

Two examiners that had not seen the writing, its notes or the hand-off checked the built
explanation (`biur.md`) against `sources/`: the מבוא and §1, and §2–§4. **The explanation's
meaning has not been changed on any finding**: each waits for Ronnie (or the next writing
session). The labels the examiners questioned were fixed (below). Paragraph numbers are those of
the review copy (`--review`): מבוא.10 = the מבוא, paragraph 10.

## Errors (the explanation says what the source does not)
1. **מבוא.10, "אין מחברים אותה אל תחתית היצירה אלא אל ראשה".** "Head to head" is right as the
   order of the prayer (SK Morning Prayers.1.2; PEC Gate of Prayer.6.13 "צריך לייחד תחלה ראש
   היצירה עם ראש העשייה"). But the negative is contradicted wherever the rising itself is
   described: SK Morning Prayers.3.5 "פנימיות ג"ר דעשיה ונעשים חיצוניות אל ג"ת דיצירה"; PEC
   Gate of Prayer.7.44, 6.14. Keep the order; drop the claim about where עשיה attaches.
2. **§1.13, how the two works count the eighteen blessings.** שער הברכות itself has both counts,
   in adjacent paragraphs (PEC Gate of Blessings.4.2 from ענ"י; 4.3 from הנותן לשכוי), and 5.1's
   heading says "עשרים ברכות של שחר" from ענט"י; "three before them" + 18 makes 21, unaddressed.
   SK is stated correctly (Morning Blessings.7, 9). Smaller: PEC Gate of Prayer.7.55/7.57 repeat
   the לשכוי count; the exceptions omit "שעשה לי כל צרכי" on ט"ב and יוה"כ; Morning Blessings.9
   puts the 18 "בז"א דעשיה", in tension with 13–14 (ענט"י = א"א, אשר יצר = אבא).

## Imprecise (true, but compressed or overstated)
3. **מבוא.19 and §1.19, the deeds and "the upper ones of the world below of themselves".** The
   upper seven are fixed in חיצוניות only; their פנימיות one step later (SK Morning Prayers.3.3–3.4;
   PEC 7.37–7.40): עשיה's by תש"י, יצירה's by תש"ר; בריאה's needs none. §1.19 needs
   "בבחינת חיצוניות בלבד" (3.3).
4. **מבוא.17–21 against PEC שער התפילה ch. 8.** Not a contradiction of the reading of SK, but of the
   generalization "המעשה מתקן את חיצוניות העולמות... ומה שהמעשה אינו עושה הוא העלייה": 8.1 has the
   deeds make מוחין "פנימי ומקיף דנשמות"; 8.2 (the editor) names the conflict with the opening of the
   gate; 8.6–8.8 give a third mapping; 8.4 "אנו מעלים עשייה ביצירה" in the קרבנות. Also, SK 3.2's
   "פנימיות" (of the sefirot) and דרוש ב''s "פנימיות" (the souls) are used without marking the switch;
   2.2 has light descend from each upper world into עשיה.
5. **מבוא.22, PEC 6.11 set in as a plain statement.** It is one of several reports R. Chaim Vital
   calls contradictory (6.8 "יש בהם דברים הפכים, ואיני זוכר תירוצם"); 6.9–6.10, 7.53 have the outer
   worlds rise on Shabbat and Yom Tov. For weekdays the reports agree.
6. **מבוא.3, "לכן ראשית התפילה היא בעשיה".** The source (SK Morning Blessings.1) says all prayer is in
   עשיה, as the מלכות's request, not its first part; "שהוא המלכות של העולמות" is an addition.
7. **§2.5 (with 2.2), the small tallit counted in עשיה and in יצירה.** The quotation leaves out the line
   just before it (SK Tzitzit.6.3 "ציצית קטן הוא בחיצו' היצירה ולא בפנימיות"), which contradicts 3.3 as
   given in 2.1; "מתיישב" settles only עשיה versus יצירה.
8. **§2.14–2.15, the four fringes and the fillings.** The four simple letters were hers from the start;
   only the fillings are given (SK Tzitzit.1.2). The passage also makes the fringes the five גבורות from
   בינה, derives the knots from the five letters of the filling, and (1.3) gives the four fillings four
   meanings (נפש for מ"ן, the souls that bring גרים, רוח, נשמה) and calls the filled name "נפש".
9. **§2.4, 2.6, 2.21, "גדלות" in two senses.** The large tallit is "גדלות" in SK Tzitzit.1.1, 6.3 and PEC
   Gate of Fringes.1.14, and "יניקה, ואין טלית ג' כנגד הגדלות" in SK Tzitzit.5.2; the explanation does not
   say the first "גדלות" is not the tefillin's.
10. **§3.22, Rashi and Rabbenu Tam.** A fair selection (Vital: "מפרש הדרך הא' ולא פליגי"), but "הגבורות
    דוחות בו את החסדים" does not follow as written: the text explains why the חסדים must re-enter
    (SK Tefillin.6.2), and limits it to "בתחילת הכנסתם".
11. **§3.18, the דרוש's own contradiction.** SK Tefillin.5.9 first raises and resolves a weekday
    contradiction (the point face to face, nine sefirot back to back), then ראש השנה, left "וצ"ע" (5.6);
    the explanation skips the first, and its closing can read as answering the second.
12. **§4.5–4.6.** Only the פנימיות of the first three of עשיה rises (3.5–3.6); the חיצוניות sentence is
    Vital's hedged "ונלע"ד חיים ששמעתי"; "ועוד שינוי אחד" assumes a first one named (the text: "ב' מיני
    שינויים").

## Minor
- מבוא.27: the union of the עמידה is sound (1.3, 7.54, 8.9, 8.13); 7.46 states positions, and 7.62 puts the
  act at נפילת אפים. מבוא.14/29: אצילות runs to the קדיש before אשרי (1.3). מבוא.8: both works carry both
  versions of היכל ק"ק דבריאה (PEC 6.13 too). מבוא.29: "מהודו עד ב"ש" is the head of עשיה, not the קרבנות.
- §1.7–1.8: PEC Gate of Blessings.5.20 qualifies the raising of עשיה at ענט"י ("לאו דוקא... ההכנה לבד").
  §1.9: "[דזו"ן]" dropped from "כנגד אבא [דזו"ן] שבעולם העשיה".
- §3.11: two reasons for the rising at dawn (Leah no longer pushes it aside; Torah after midnight); one given.
  §3.13: the reading "מתוך אהבתו אותה" is from SK Tefillin.7.2, not דרוש ה' (5.4, 5.6: her plea).
- §2.17: the text's reason the תכלת does not hold back the לבן is two kinds of fringes (SK Tzitzit.4.7).
