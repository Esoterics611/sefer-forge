# open questions (level 2, after §1–§4)

1. **15b.1 vs 24b.6** (the טלית גדול): 15b.1 has the tallitot separate the קליפות from "חיצוניות דז' עליונות דכחב"ד חג"ת
   דיציר'", while 24b.6 / 16a.12 have the טלית גדול fix the upper seven of **עשיה**, and 15b.2 gives the upper seven of יצירה to
   תש"י. Probably דעשיה in 15b.1; the explanation follows 24b.6 and does not mention 15b.1's wording.
2. **The days, "דשבוע שעבר / דשבוע הבא"** (25a.2–7): he does not say why the first three days belong to the past week and the
   last three to the next. The explanation states it without a reason.
3. **20b.6 vs 19a.10**: 20b.6 puts the blessings of mitzvot in the inner worlds (מעשיות = חיצוניות דפנימיות, דיבור = פנימיות
   דפנימיות) against the blessings of enjoyment in the outer; 19a.10 / 15a.7 put the deeds in the outer worlds. Two frames
   (mitzvah vs enjoyment; deed vs speech). Not set in.
4. **16a.8 vs 17a.1**: one passage gives the קטן the mochin of קטנות to the outer partzuf and the גדול גדלות to the inner; 17a.1
   concludes each tallit draws עיבור, יניקה and גדלות to all three partzufim of its layer. The explanation follows 17a.1.
5. **24b.1** "י"ב פרצופים הכוללים" with nine pairs listed (24b.2).
6. The **"עשרים ברכות"** of PEC Gate of Blessings.5.1 is not addressed by the Rashash in what was read.
7. **His siddur's charts** are not available; the names are explained from his prose (15a–17b). Where a chart would be needed:
   the exact pointing of the הויות and אהי"ה per world and per partzuf.
8. **Level 1, for Ronnie**: (a) its check finding 7 (the small tallit in עשיה/יצירה, inner/outer) is resolved by 16a.6 and 24b.6;
   (b) finding 4 (PEC Prayer ch. 8) is reconciled by the Rashash's two layers; (c) its §1.13 count of the 18: the Rashash calls the
   count from ענ"י in SK a scribal error (46a.5, 46a.7). Level 1 itself was not changed on any of these.
9. **The copy's slips.** The grimoar text of נהר שלום has many one-letter slips (נמות for נפשות, הכים for הכלים ...).
   Inside a printed passage they are marked with `emend=` ("[צ״ל: ...]"); `check.md` lists those done. A full
   proofreading of the copy against a printed edition is not possible here (no scan).
