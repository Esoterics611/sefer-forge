# argument-map (level 2, the Rashash) — מבוא and §1–§4 written 2026-11-15

## מבוא: five foundations
1. **The chain of sons** (R1 3b.1): Israel → זו"ן → ישסו"ת → או"א → א"א → עתיק; all depends on זו"ן, זו"ן on Israel; every day new sparks.
2. **The measure of weekday prayer** (4a.6, 4b.1, 4b.4; 13b.2): weekdays to ישסו"ת, נרנח"י דנשמה, to the chest of א"א; Shabbat
   שחרית/מוסף או"א (חיה, throat), מנחה א"א (יחידה); weekday = the אחור; union of יעקב ורחל, not of זו"ן (= level 1 PEC 7.46).
   The days (25a.2–10): one "king" a day, each in its world; each day sifts its own; Shabbat in אצילות. 24a.1–2: אב"א over a day,
   פב"פ the next, general union at נפילת אפים of the third day; the night's descent.
3. **The layers of a world** (12a.3–8; 13b.6; 13b.8–9): פנימיות = souls (נפשות, רוחות, נשמות), חיצוניות = אופנים, מלאכים,
   שרפים/כסא; each 5 partzufim × 5 = נרנח"י; four layers; שחרית in the inner ABYA.
4. **Deed and speech** (15a.7; 19a.10): deeds = outer and inner of the outer ABYA, in place; speech = outer and inner of the
   inner ABYA, raising world in world and the outer with them. PEC Prayer 8.1 (עולמות/נשמות) = the two unions.
5. **Two unions, two mochin** (20a.4–20b.1); **what is drawn** (9b.2: צלם, mochin, pointed הויות); **the יחוד per world**
   (15a.8 ff.: same-position letter of הוי"ה, אדנ"י, אהי"ה).
