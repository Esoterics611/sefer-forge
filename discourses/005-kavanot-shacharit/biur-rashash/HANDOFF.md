## What it is
Level 2: how the Rashash reads and extends the Arizal's kavanot of Shacharit, from his own
words in נהר שלום (with הקדמת רחובות הנהר). It assumes level 1 and does not repeat it:
§N here opens from what §N of level 1 established and says what the Rashash adds, changes
or makes precise: the inner and outer worlds (אבי"ע דפנימיות / דחיצוניות), the ten
partzufim and נרנח"י of every partzuf, the four repairs of prayer (ד' תיקוני התפלה), the
days of the week, the mochin of each stage. נהר שלום has nothing on stages 11–13.
